## Abstract
A reactor that runs for weeks with nobody beside it is only as good as its own record, so every reading here shows when it has stopped being true.
BR-01 has grown B. subtilis five times, and the longest run logged 434 hours at 22 °C with no sample drawn. Four results carry a second, independent check. A reference beam that never sees the culture separates lamp drift from growth. Wall shear is computed twice, from pressure and from flow. The photometer is scored against a known TiO₂ dose. The field leaf sensor is scored against an air probe in the same air. Replayed over the log, the trust layer raised its alarm 26.7 hours before the OD channel failed, on one event in one run, and it comes with the 434-hour batch as its test case for any team with an optical sensor. A growth forecast fixed by hash before the 8 October batch is scored on it. Both interfaces run live on this page, and both are under the MIT licence. The loop is not closed, and almost every result rests on one run.
## Key results
- Trust layer · Worked · 26.7 h of warning before the OD channel failed
- Two-route cross-check · Worked · Wall shear from pressure and from flow agree to 2.8% at the operating point
- Batch forecast · Worked · Kalman beats persistence by 41% at 2 h ahead
- Plate-to-reactor prior · Partial · 24 h biomass within 7% on held-out arms
- Photometer calibration · Calibrated · Slope 1.018 against the BioDrop to OD 1.2, R² 0.995; both also scored against a known dose
- Field leaf sensor · Bench · Reads 1.2 to 1.9 °C warm against the same air; wetness untested
- Registered forecast · Pending · Growth rate and shape fixed by hash before the batch
