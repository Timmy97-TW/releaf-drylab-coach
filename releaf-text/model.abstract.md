## Abstract
The model turns Taiwan’s weather into a stress forecast, the forecast into a lamp setting, and the reactor’s measured output into litres per hectare and New Taiwan dollars per hectare per year.
Three blocks run as one chain, each tested against something outside itself. Taiwan’s own rice harvests since 2001 chose the stress index: 32,767 models were scored on years they had not seen, and the one kept has 32% less error than the no-weather trend in the second crop. Our DNPH assay sets reactor output at 1.056 g of ACC cleared per litre per day, from one replicate above detection and below quantitation. The one dose this project has applied to living plants is 1,670 L/ha on a seedling plate, which a rice canopy multiplies to 4,008 L of culture per hectare per application, diluted into 400 L of spray. The weather decides how often: 17 of 25 seasons never fire at all, and a season that does takes nine passes. Capital returns in 2.4 years on salt-affected coastal land; on an ordinary paddy it does not return in 64.7.
